Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 21, 2024

1. Introduction

Ask My Analytics ("we", "our", or "us"), a service provided by Get to Revenue LLC, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service to connect Google Analytics 4 (GA4) data with ChatGPT Custom GPTs.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you sign in with Google, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Google account ID (sub)
  • Profile information provided by Google

2.2 Google Analytics Data

To provide analytics query services, we access:

  • GA4 property information (property ID, name)
  • OAuth refresh tokens (encrypted and stored securely)
  • Analytics data that you query through the Custom GPT (read-only access)

2.3 Usage Data

We log query requests for debugging and service improvement:

  • Query parameters (metrics, dimensions, date ranges)
  • Response summaries
  • Error logs

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide the analytics connector service
  • Authenticate your account and manage access
  • Process and respond to your GA4 data queries
  • Improve our service and debug issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption: OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted using Fernet encryption before storage
  • Secure Transmission: All data is transmitted over HTTPS
  • Database Security: Access to stored data is restricted and monitored
  • Token Security: Access tokens are hashed before storage

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data:

  • With Google Analytics API to fetch your analytics data (as authorized by you)
  • With ChatGPT when you make queries through the Custom GPT (via OAuth tokens)
  • As required by law or legal process

6. OAuth and Third-Party Services

Our service uses OAuth 2.0 to securely access:

  • Google Sign-In: For user authentication
  • Google Analytics API: For read-only access to your GA4 data
  • ChatGPT Custom GPT: Via OAuth tokens for API access

These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your stored data
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Revoke Access: Disconnect Google Analytics or revoke OAuth tokens at any time
  • Export: Export your data before deletion

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address provided in Section 9.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Query logs are retained for debugging purposes but can be deleted upon request. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information and encrypted tokens, except where retention is required by law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
